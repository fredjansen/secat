Disclaimer

This catalog is a research artifact. Read the limitations and threats to validity below before using its data in other studies or decisions.

Limitations of the online catalog

AI-generated answers

Every answer was produced by an AI model (Claude) from its training knowledge, without live access to the sites. Answers may be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated, and have not yet been fully validated by human experts.

Snapshot in time

Grey literature sources change constantly: features, login methods, monetization and moderation policies evolve, and some sites close or become read-only. The data reflects the state known when it was generated (October 2026).

Missing quantitative data

Most statistical facets (daily audience, registered users, percentage answered, interactions, citations) are not publicly reported in a comparable way and are marked as not available. The few numbers shown are approximate.

Closed set of values

Answers are restricted to the expected values defined in the facet classification. Characteristics that do not fit those values are lost or approximated by the closest value.

Heterogeneous sources

The list mixes whole platforms, sub-communities (subreddits, Stack Exchange sites) and features of platforms (GitHub Issues and Discussions), so some facets describe the hosting platform rather than the specific community.

No endorsement

Listing a source does not imply endorsement of its content. External sites are the responsibility of their owners, and trademarks belong to their respective holders.

Threats to validity

Following the classification commonly adopted in empirical software engineering (Wohlin et al., 2012; Runeson & Höst, 2009):

Construct validity

  • Facets such as tone of discussions, quality level of answers and experience level are subjective and were not operationalized with objective measurement criteria.
  • Some facets may not capture the concept they intend to measure; for example, registration methods does not distinguish between methods required to read and methods required to post.
  • Boolean facets (API, dashboard, app) do not distinguish between features of the community and features of the company that hosts it.

Internal validity

  • The AI model may produce plausible but incorrect answers (hallucinations), especially for small or less-known sites.
  • Answers were produced by a single automated rater, without inter-rater agreement or independent cross-checking.
  • The model's knowledge has a cutoff date and may not reflect recent changes in the sites.

External validity

  • The 32 sources were selected by the researchers and do not form a random or exhaustive sample of grey literature in software engineering.
  • The sample favours English-language, Q&A and community sites; blogs, videos, podcasts and company documentation are under-represented, so the results may not generalize to other types of grey literature.

Conclusion validity

  • Because many quantitative facets are not available, comparisons between sources based on statistics are not reliable.
  • The small number of sources per platform type limits any generalization about platform types.

Reliability

  • Running the same questions again with an AI model may produce different answers. To mitigate this, all inputs (site list, facets and questions) and outputs are published in JSON and can be exported from the Export page.
  • Each source has a confidence level and a list of references that allows independent verification.

How to report a problem

If you find incorrect or outdated information, please let us know through the Feedback page.
